🖋️ HTML Cheque Writer & Printer

A lightweight, completely offline, and standalone web application for writing, aligning, and printing cheques with millimeter precision.

This tool is designed to run entirely in your web browser without the need for external servers, databases, or complex installations. All your financial data, payee history, and layout templates are saved locally on your device for maximum privacy and security.
✨ Features

    Drag-and-Drop Calibration: Easily click and drag text fields (Date, Payee, Amount in Numbers, Amount in Words, etc.) across a virtual canvas to perfectly align with your physical bank cheques.

    Auto Number-to-Words: Automatically converts numerical inputs (e.g., 1250.50) into formatted text phrasing (e.g., One Thousand Two Hundred Fifty and 50/100).

    Security Formatting: Optional security padding to wrap numbers in asterisks (e.g., ***1250.50***) and add crossing lines (e.g., "A/C Payee Only") to prevent fraud.

    Template Management: Upload a scanned image of your blank cheque as a background guide to help position your text. Once aligned, save the coordinate layout as a preset template for future use.

    Precision Printing: Built-in @media print CSS rules ensure that when you press print, all UI elements and background images are hidden, and only the text is printed onto your physical cheque at the exact coordinates specified.

    Cheque Register Ledger: Maintains a local history of issued cheques. Features auto-incrementing serial numbers, the ability to void cheques, and a 1-click export to CSV for your accounting software.

    Currency & Memo Free: Clean, generalized interface stripped of specific currency symbols and memo fields for a streamlined, universal layout.
